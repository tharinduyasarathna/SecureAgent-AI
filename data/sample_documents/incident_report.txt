
SecureAgent AI - Sample Security Incident Report

Incident ID: SEC-2026-001
Severity: High
Category: Suspicious Authentication Activity

Summary:
Multiple failed login attempts were detected against an
administrative account from an unfamiliar IP address.

Observations:
The monitoring system recorded 45 failed authentication
attempts within five minutes.

Potential Threat:
The activity may indicate a brute-force password attack.

Recommended Actions:
1. Review authentication logs.
2. Investigate the source IP address.
3. Verify whether the account was compromised.
4. Enable multi-factor authentication.
5. Apply appropriate account lockout policies.

Status:
Investigation pending.
